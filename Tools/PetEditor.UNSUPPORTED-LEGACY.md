# PetEditor is unsupported legacy source

`PetEditor.sln` and the `PetEditor/` tree are preserved only for historical reference. They use
legacy `packages.config` restoration and are not built, tested, packaged, or supported by the
DesktopPet AI Edition release pipeline.

Do not treat a successful IDE load as product validation. The maintained validation utility is
[`PetTester.sln`](PetTester.sln).
